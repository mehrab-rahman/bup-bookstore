## Notes

- Book pairs are editorial study suggestions, not claims of required official reading or latest editions. Source links document the books or BUP reference lists; a source from another FBS program supports bibliographic identity, not official assignment to this course.
- Four departments use every entry under the current official eight semester headers. BUP mixes syllabus versions and elective/specialization pools, which are retained with notes.
- Management uses historical official indexed entries because its current course section is empty. Semester assignment is explicitly provisional.
- Internship and viva are retained when source-listed. The other four current course sections do not list internship/viva entries, so none have been invented.
- Tax and law texts are study references; the applicable current legislation and instructor-prescribed edition must be checked.
- Course codes, titles, and semester grouping were transcribed from the six public BUP undergraduate program pages, retrieved 10 October 2026. Official verification applies to the source-listed curriculum, not to all suggested books.
- All source-listed entries are retained, including viva examinations, research, laboratory and fieldwork courses, apparent duplicate titles, and optional-looking later-semester entries. The website does not consistently distinguish compulsory and elective courses; this catalog is not a credit/registration audit.
- Each book has recommendationType: official-reference when identifiable in that course reference section; editor-suggestion otherwise. Suggestions use real published books. Supplementary book identity is linked to publisher, author, or library/book records; cross-course suggestions are not official prescriptions. The Criminal Justice in Bangladesh reference is supported by the BUP Public Administration bibliography.
- Repeated Gender and Development (Development semesters 4 and 5) and Moral Philosophy (Sociology semesters 2 and 4), plus unusual English viva placement, follow the published source rather than silently correcting it.
- Broad anthologies support literary-period courses; read the relevant period selections. For oral examinations and research projects, research/writing and viva preparation books are supplementary skill resources, not substitutes for the courses being examined.
- French resources are suggested where the official foreign-language reference section points to French. Confirm the language actually assigned to the student cohort.
- Book titles and author names have been normalized where source bibliography spelling is inconsistent. Edition years and availability are deliberately not asserted.
- Retrieved official BUP degree pages on 2026-10-10. Semester/course entries reproduce each page’s Course List; this does not establish which curriculum version applies to a particular student cohort.
- All book mappings are editorial suggested reading, not a claim of a required BUP purchase list. Most titles are normalized from BUP course references; gaps use relevant reading from another BUP course or a verified publisher/author source.
- Laboratories, fieldwork, internships, projects, theses and oral examinations are retained. Their books are supporting or revision reading, not evidence that BUP prescribes textbooks for those activities.
- Edition numbers are generally omitted because source citations contain inconsistent editions and typographic errors. Verify the edition with the course instructor.
- CSE has conflicting Outline and Course List versions. Main semesters use the named Course List (75 entries); the separate published Outline is preserved in cse-alternate-outline.json and is not combined into compulsory requirements.
- PCHR lists Arabic, Chinese and French alternatives in semester 4. Law and MCJ contain duplicated titles with versioned codes, retained with notes.
- Semester/course placement follows the supplied PDF, not the current BUP website.
- Semester 8 elective/stream status needs batch confirmation.
- Foreign-language choice is unspecified; general language-learning books supplied.
- Unlinked book metadata is a draft bibliography pending independent publisher-source verification; existing linked CSE references were reused where appropriate.
